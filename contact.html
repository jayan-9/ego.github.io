Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a reply?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on business days (Monday–Friday). If you contact us on a weekend, we'll get back to you by the next business day. Please check your spam folder if you don't receive a reply within 48 hours.

Can I suggest new name styles or symbols?

Absolutely! We love community input. Just send us a message with your ideas – including the style name and example – and we'll consider adding it in our next update. Many of our 100+ styles came from user suggestions!

Do you offer custom name style creation?

Currently, we focus on our free public tool with 100+ pre-made styles. For enterprise, bulk, or custom requests, please email us at stylenamein@gmail.com with your requirements. We'll get back to you to discuss options.

Is stylename.in really free? Are there any hidden charges?

Yes, 100% free with no hidden charges! We monetize through Google AdSense only, which allows us to keep the tool free for everyone. You'll never be asked for payment or premium subscription.

How do I report a bug or technical issue?

Use our contact form and select "Bug Report" in the subject field. Please include as much detail as possible: what you were doing, what happened, and your device/browser. Screenshots are very helpful!

Can I use stylename.in for commercial purposes?

Yes! All generated names are free to use for personal, commercial, and business purposes. You can use them for gaming channels, social media branding, product names, and more. No attribution required.

Do you store my data or generated names?

No. We don't store any user data or generated content. Your privacy is our top priority. The only data we collect is anonymous usage statistics for improving our service. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
