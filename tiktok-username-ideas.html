6. ✨ How to Make These Names YOURS

If you liked a name but it's already taken, don't panic! Use these simple tricks and make that name yours:

  • 1. Add an underscore or dot: @Couch.Conspiracy_ or @Couch.Conspiracy.exe
  • 2. Add a number with meaning: @NotUrAvgMochi_99 (use your birth year or lucky number like 23, 07, 99).
  • 3. Use "Its" or "The": @ItsThePickleRick or @TheCouchConspiracy
  • 4. Add "TV" or "Tok": @PickleRickTok or @CouchConspiracyTV
  • 5. Add your initials: @CR_CouchConspiracy or @PickleRick_AB

With these easy tricks, you can make any taken name your own.
