💡 Tips & Tricks for Korean Style Names

📝 Keep It Short

Korean names look best when they are 5-15 characters long. Shorter names are easier to remember and look cleaner.

🎭 Mix Korean and English

Combining Korean words with English names creates a unique fusion style that stands out. Example: 사랑 (Sarang) + Queen.

✨ Use Decorative Symbols

Symbols like ♡, ✿, 𖹭, ꧁, ꧂ make Korean names look more attractive and authentic. Don't overdo it – 2-3 symbols are enough.

🔢 Add Numbers for Modern Touch

Numbers like ⁹⁹⁹, ⁰⁰⁷, ⁴⁴⁴ make Korean names look trendy and modern. Use them at the beginning or end of your name.

🎯 Choose a Theme

Decide on a theme – Love, Gaming, Royal, Dark, Cute – and choose words and symbols that match that theme.
