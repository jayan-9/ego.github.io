2. Important Things to Keep in Mind While Choosing a Free Fire Name

Many players make mistakes while choosing a name, which results in their name getting banned or looking absolutely ugly. Keep these points in mind:

  • Don't use abusive or offensive words: Such names get banned quickly in Free Fire. Keep your identity clean and classy. After all, you are not a shameless person, are you? So keep a good and unique stylish name.
  • Don't keep a very long name: A name longer than 15-20 characters doesn't display properly in the game and often gets cut off. Nowadays, Free Fire doesn't even support such long names properly.
  • Don't add too many symbols: 2-3 symbols are more than enough. Too many symbols make the name difficult to read and it looks messy and trashy. It simply doesn't look good.
